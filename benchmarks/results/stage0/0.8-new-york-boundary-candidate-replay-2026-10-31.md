from the New York demo start. They were staged locally from the HPI Overpass

- `.cell-refresh-staging/0.8-new-york-boundary-south-2026-10-31/`

server at `http://localhost:3456` and visible Chrome 156 ran one 12-second
eastbound walk and one 12-second drive from `40.758, -73.9855`. Chrome selected
the NVIDIA RTX 4060 on the i7-13650HX host, not the target i5-1035G1 laptop.
Both captures used clean source revision `f3e3f47`.
