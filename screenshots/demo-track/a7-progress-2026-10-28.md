These are single 300-frame samples, so A7 remains open: the Tokyo sample exceeds

and Tokyo streams also had unresolved neighboring cells in both versions; the
same neighbor keys failed on both sides. Visual spot checks showed the same
street framing in each matched city pair. Shader compilation errors were absent
in both versions; existing geometry merge warnings and the local assets 501
were present in both. The six longer Edinburgh measurements are in the
[repeat reports](rehearsal-2026-10-28-a7-edinburgh-repeats/).
